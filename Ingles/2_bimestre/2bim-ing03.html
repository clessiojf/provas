📝 Inglês | Simulado Dissertativo – SELLEP (Units 4, 5, 6)

Simulado Dissertativo – 6 Questões

Responda em inglês. Use frases completas.

Unit 4 – Daily Routine

1) Describe your morning routine using complete sentences. Include at least three actions and the times you do them.

2) Explain why having a consistent daily routine is important. Give examples.

Unit 5 – Frequency

3) Write about how often you do different activities during the week. Use frequency expressions.

4) Explain why frequency expressions are important when talking about habits.

Unit 6 – Physical Description

5) Describe a person you know (real or fictional). Include hair, eyes, height and build.

6) Explain the difference between using “to be” and “to have” in physical descriptions. Give examples.
