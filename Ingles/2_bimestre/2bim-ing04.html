Simulado Dissertativo 2 – 6 Questões

Responda em inglês. Use frases completas.

Unit 4 – Daily Routine

1) Describe your evening routine. Write at least three actions you usually do after school or before going to bed, including the times.

2) Explain how your routine changes on weekends. Compare your weekday routine with your weekend routine.

Unit 5 – Frequency

3) Write about how often your family does different activities together. Use frequency expressions.

4) Explain how frequency expressions help you talk about your habits more clearly. Give examples.

Unit 6 – Physical Description

5) Describe a famous person or character. Include hair, eyes, height, build, and other physical details.

6) Explain why it is important to use the correct order of adjectives in English. Give at least two examples.
